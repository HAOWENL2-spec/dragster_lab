How to use

1. Select points: click two or more neighbouring dots on the same outline. Click Curve, then drag the single middle dot left, right, inward or outward to bend the whole selection; both ends stay fixed. Straight removes the curve.

2. Drag points: click to select, or drag a dot directly. The coordinate panel shows X from the front, Y upwards from the original timber's lower edge in Top view, and Z above the base in Side view. Type a value to adjust it. In Curve mode, use the single middle dot instead. Click Finish when done to leave editing. Reset timber restores the starting body; Undo brings your edits back. Point count stays fixed. Curves stay inside the stock and clear of the rear hole. Axles do not restrict body editing.

3. Symmetric / Asymmetric: symmetric mode mirrors your next top-view edits and cuts across the centre line. Asymmetric mode changes only the chosen side.

Trim length: start with 305 mm timber. Choose Trim length and drag the orange line, or enter the length to keep (180–305 mm). Cut from the front; the rear hole is preserved. Shortening removes wood instead of stretching the shape. Choose From bottom to drag a horizontal line upward in Side view, or enter its height from the original base. The rear hole and axle supports are protected. Reset timber restores 305 mm.

Axles: click Axles once to add both axles and show their positions here. Drag the markers or type positions measured from the nose. Remove axles returns to a body-only view. Body editing never locks to axle positions. There is no 60%-of-length rule: only a small gap between wheels. The Front axle check and Rear axle check update automatically. Both must pass before saving or racing. Each checks its mounting area, wheel clearance and its position relative to the estimated balance point. These are classroom model checks; real construction still needs inspection.

4. Select area → Remove: drag a rectangle and check the highlighted area. Choose a depth in millimetres or Through, then Remove. Top cuts go down; side cuts go in from the shown side. Selections may extend outside the timber; only overlapping wood is removed. For a smooth or pointed nose, choose Cut shape in Top view, then adjust End and Remove. In Side view, drag the left/right walls to change the cut range and the bottom line to change depth. The round end handles set different front and rear depths. Type values for precise changes. Click Update cut to apply, then Finish. Click an existing cut in Select area to edit it again. Both views and the 3D model show applied cuts. Undo restores wood.

5. Save car: add one short change note. Select 1–4 saved cars to Race. Click a Record to reopen the model. Tick records, then Clear selected records to delete those cars. Clear all records empties Record and the race lanes in one click. Undo clear restores them until the next save or page reload; the current body design is kept.

Export PDF: download the current Top and Side views with point coordinates, axle positions and cut details. This is a drawing for keeping or printing; it cannot reopen the editable model.

Session only: Save car and Record last until you close or reload this page. Each new session starts with fresh timber.
